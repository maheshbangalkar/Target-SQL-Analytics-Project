# Business Requirements – Summary

Target operates a rapidly growing e-commerce business in Brazil, generating a large
amount of transactional data. However, raw data by itself does not clearly show how
the business is performing in terms of orders, revenue, logistics, and customer behavior.

The purpose of this project is to **convert historical e-commerce data into meaningful
and actionable business insights**, helping stakeholders make better decisions for
business growth.

---

### Why This Project Was Needed
- To understand how the number of orders has increased or decreased over time
- To analyze how different states and regions are performing
- To evaluate delivery speed and overall logistics efficiency
- To track revenue growth and understand the impact of shipping (freight) costs
- To analyze customer payment preferences and installment usage

---

### What the Business Wanted to Achieve
- Gain clear visibility into overall e-commerce performance
- Identify delays and issues in operations and delivery processes
- Reduce costs and improve delivery timelines
- Use data to support better regional and business-level planning
- Improve customer experience through faster and more reliable deliveries

---

### How This Project Helps
By analyzing historical data from **2016 to 2018** using SQL, this project enables the business to:
- Clearly understand platform performance
- Get reliable insights into delivery timelines and shipping costs
- Identify patterns in regional performance and customer payment behavior

---

### Business Value
This analysis helps Target move away from guesswork and adopt **data-driven decision-making**.
As a result, the business can improve operational efficiency, increase customer satisfaction,
and plan more effectively for long-term growth.
